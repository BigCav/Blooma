Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need a contract?

No. Blooma is a month-to-month subscription with no lock-in contract. You can manage or cancel your plan any time from your venue dashboard.

What if I already use another booking system?

You can set up your services, staff and hours in Blooma in about 10 minutes and run it alongside your current system until you're ready to switch over fully.

Do my customers need the Blooma app?

No app required. Your booking page works straight from a browser, perfect for sharing as a link in your Instagram bio, texts or Google Business profile.

How does the referral bonus work?

Every Blooma venue gets a unique referral link from their dashboard. When a venue you refer subscribes to a paid plan, you both get $120 credited to your Blooma wallet automatically.

What happens after my free trial?

You get 7 days to try Blooma with no credit card required. After that, choose a plan to keep going. Your venue, services and booking history are all still there waiting for you.
