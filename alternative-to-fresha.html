Frequently asked

Is Blooma cheaper than Fresha?

In most cases, yes. Blooma's Solo plan is $19/month flat versus Fresha's $44.95 + GST, and Blooma's card processing (1.79% + $0.20) undercuts Fresha's (2.49% + $0.30). Fresha also adds a one-time 20% commission (minimum $7) on new clients who book through its marketplace, which Blooma doesn't charge.

Can I move my services and staff from Fresha to Blooma?

Yes. You can set up your services, staff, hours and pricing in Blooma in about 10 minutes, and run it alongside Fresha until you're ready to switch fully.

Does Blooma have a client marketplace like Fresha?

Blooma includes a local marketplace where customers can discover your venue, with no commission charged on those bookings.
