Questions

Frequently asked

Is Blooma cheaper than Timely?

For a solo stylist, Blooma is $24/month flat. Timely's cheapest plan is priced per bookable staff member, so cost rises as your team grows. For a team of several stylists, it's worth comparing Timely's per-staff plan cost against Blooma's $19 per stylist Team pricing directly.

Can I move my services and staff from Timely to Blooma?

Yes. You can set up your services, staff, hours and pricing in Blooma in about 10 minutes, and run it alongside Timely until you're ready to switch fully.

Is Blooma a New Zealand company?

Yes, Blooma is built for New Zealand venues, with pricing in NZD and a marketplace focused on New Zealand customers.
